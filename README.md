# Favorite Series
**Ri-chan**
*Yamamoto Koudai*
>Ri-chan by yamamoto koudai is a slice of life manga that explores the complexities of growing up and reconnecting with the past.
>The manga portrays how time and experience can shape people in unexpected ways. It reflects on the bittersweet nature of nostalgia.
>The story’s calm pacing and realistic dialogue allow readers to feel the emotional distance and gradual reconnection between the two characters.
>Ri-chan is not just about rekindled friendship or romance; it is a reflection on change, memory, and acceptance. It reminds readers that people evolve, and that understanding who someone has become is just as important as remembering who they once were.
